About Me

I build things
that actually ship.

I'm a full-stack Python developer based in Kenya. But more than just writing code, I deploy, operate, and own production systems. My flagship project EasyHostels isn't a GitHub repo — it's a live marketplace with 150+ active users, running on a self-managed Ubuntu VPS I configured from scratch: Nginx, Cloudflare DNS, PostgreSQL, custom SSL. When something breaks at 2am, I fix it.

"I take full ownership — from the first line of code to production incidents. I don't hand off deployment to someone else."

My interest in AI isn't theoretical. At the Mozilla Responsible Computing Challenge, I ranked 2nd out of 30 teams building an AI-powered agricultural decision support system — real ML applied to real-world food security problems. Shortly after, a client hired me solo to build InvoiceIQ, an LLM-powered invoice extraction platform. I delivered it in 4 weeks: OCR pipeline, Llama 3.3 integration, Human-in-the-Loop review dashboard, DRF API — the whole thing.

I'm currently finishing my BSc IT at the University of Embu, where I've balanced coursework with shipping production code. I believe the best developers close the loop — they don't just build features, they understand how they behave at scale, under real traffic, with real users.

Production-first thinking I design for the deployed state, not the demo.
