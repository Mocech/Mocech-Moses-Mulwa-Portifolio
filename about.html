About MoxienTech

Reliable software,
delivered on time.

I'm Moses Mulwa, a software developer and the founder of MoxienTech, based in Nairobi and Embu, Kenya. I help organisations, businesses and communities get the website or app they need without long waits or inflated costs. You tell me the problem, and I take care of the rest: planning, building, putting it online and keeping it running.

"You shouldn't have to worry about missed deadlines or surprise costs. I agree the work upfront, deliver when I say I will, and stay around after launch."

I've done this for real people. The University of Embu Christian Union website, built with a fellow developer, brings a whole community's ministries, events and elections into one place. EasyHostels helped students find verified housing, with over 150 people using it. When a client needed a tool to read invoices automatically, I delivered it in 4 weeks, working alone.

Delivered on time An invoice-reading tool went from brief to live in 4 weeks, built solo.
Fair, friendly pricing Costs are agreed upfront, so there are no surprises later.
Plain, simple communication No jargon. Clear updates and quick replies.
Support after launch I stay available after launch to keep things running and fix problems.
